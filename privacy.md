# Privacy Policy: BTB n8n

Last updated: 3 November 2026

"BTB n8n" is the Google OAuth app that the Build Then Break YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@BuildThenBreak) uses to connect its own self-hosted n8n workflows to its own Google account (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Calendar and Google Drive).

## Who uses it
Only Build Then Break, for its own account. It is not offered as a service to other people.

## What data it accesses
Only the data of the Google account that signs in: emails, sheets, calendar events and files that the n8n workflows shown on the channel read or write (for example labelling an email, adding a row to a sheet, or booking a calendar slot).

## How data is used and stored
- Data is processed by n8n running on the channel's own computer. It is not sold, shared or sent to third parties, except the AI model a workflow calls (for example Google Gemini) to classify or draft a reply.
- No data is used for advertising.
- Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access
The account owner can remove access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
